# Safety and compliance boundary

This starter is restricted to free-to-play entertainment.

- Demo credits are fictional, non-transferable, non-purchasable, and not redeemable.
- No deposits, cash-outs, wagering, cash prizes, or prize-value mechanics are included.
- Do not advertise demo credits as money or imply guaranteed winnings.
- Add age-appropriate access controls and privacy protections before public release.
- Before considering any real-money feature, obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice and verify licensing, age and geolocation controls, payment rules, responsible-play safeguards, security reviews, and independent game testing.
- International availability does not by itself make an offering lawful in every country.
